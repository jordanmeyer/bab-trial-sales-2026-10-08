CSV format and limits

Columns must be date,region,product,quantity,unit_price,unit_cost in that order. Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, whole quantities (0–1,000,000), and nonnegative unit amounts up to $1,000,000 with at most two decimals. Quote names containing commas or quotes. Every field is required; an invalid file is rejected as a whole.
